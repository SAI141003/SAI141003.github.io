About

Home-style Bangladeshi food in Vancouver.

We cook the dishes we grew up with: teheri, polao, kosha, macher jhol, vorta, and nasta. Everything is made to order in our kitchen on E 54th Avenue.

Order for yourself, your family, or a larger group. Weekly menus and catering are available if you book ahead.
