Add & manage photos

New photos in data/gallery.json show on the home page slideshow, menu scroll, gallery, and menu page automatically.

How to add a new photo

  1. Save your image as PNG or JPG in the folder images/gallery/ (use a simple name like beef-rezala.png).
  2. Use the form on the right to add title, description, and price, then click Add to list.
  3. Click Download gallery.json and replace data/gallery.json in your project.
  4. Upload both the image file and gallery.json to GitHub, then push (same as updating the website).

On a computer with Node installed you can instead run: node scripts/sync-gallery.js after dropping files in images/gallery/.
